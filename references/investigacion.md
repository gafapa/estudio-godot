# Reference research

Choose enough games to answer the question; 8-12 is a starting range for a new game, fewer for a narrow feature. Cover the theme, central mechanic and player fantasy. Include the Director's references, a direct competitor where one exists, and a useful counterexample.

Search current sources and open pages supporting exact claims. Prefer developer documentation, talks and postmortems; distinguish player complaints and fan explanations from verified rules. Record URLs, dates and why each source matters. Mark unverified domain facts explicitly. Do not infer a video was watched from its title or summary.

Write `docs/referencias/<topic>.md`:

| Game and category | Relevant pattern | Evidence | Adopt and adapt | Avoid | Later |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Finish with actionable lessons and gaps. Distinguish evidence from design inference. Give reviewers concrete criteria rather than exhaustive summaries. Reuse sufficient research; refresh claims that may have changed. No dependency on another local game project is required.
