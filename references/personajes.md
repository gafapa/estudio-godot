# Characters, art and sound

Start from gameplay loops. Abstract games can describe entities instead of characters. Research function, silhouette, state readability, variation, personality, animation, attachment and complaints. Cite evidence in `docs/referencias/personajes.md`.

For each entity in `docs/design/personajes.md`, specify function, player decisions, states, shape, palette, minimum animations, variants, production method and screenshot or silhouette checks. Add narrative where needed.

Maintain `docs/arte/guia_estilo.md`: references, palette, shape language, detail, typography, interface feedback, sound, music and performance budgets. Plan assets producible with available tools: original work, procedural geometry, shaders, reusable bases plus variants, or compatible licensed assets. Record every external asset and license. Do not promise complex art production without an available workflow.

For production and verification, read [technical art](../assets/plantilla/docs/guides/technical-art.md) for asset/import standards and measured rendering budgets, or [audio](../assets/plantilla/docs/guides/audio.md) for event mapping, concurrency, mixing and lifecycle checks. Use only relevant checks and distinguish proposed budgets from target-hardware measurements.
